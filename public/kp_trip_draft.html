🛵 2D1N Motorbike Trip Plan: Phnom Penh → Kampot → Bokor → Kep

🏨 Stay: Kampot Sunny Hotel

Budget-friendly, good for sharing, central location in Kampot.

📅 Day 1: Phnom Penh → Kampot → Bokor → Kep

6:00 AM: Depart Phnom Penh (direct). Arrive Kampot 9:30-10:00 AM.

☕ Breakfast/coffee in Kampot or Bokor.

⛰️ Visit Bokor Mountain: Lok Yeay Mao, old church, viewpoints.

😴 Afternoon check-in and rest.

🌅 Evening: Kep beach sunset and seafood dinner.

📅 Day 2: Kampot → Phnom Penh

🌄 Morning coffee and chill in Kampot.

10-11 AM: Depart Kampot, arrive Phnom Penh 2-3 PM.

💰 Budget (per person)

Fuel: $6-$8
Hotel: $3-$6
Food: $10-$16
Snacks: $3-$5
Total: $22-$35

⚠️ Tips

Start early, ride safely, bring raincoat, jacket, power bank, and cash.
